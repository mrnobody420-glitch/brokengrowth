What we are building

Stability from the first day forward

Broken Growth Ministries combines practical support with accountability and opportunity. Our first planned residential facility will serve adults age 18 and older, beginning with men and expanding in later phases to women and families.

⌂

Housing

Safe transitional tiny homes designed to replace instability with structure, dignity, and a place to rebuild.

⚒

Employment

Job connections, hands-on trade pathways, skills development, and support toward long-term advancement.

◎

Whole-person support

Mentorship, life skills, resource navigation, faith and values, recovery support, and a dependable community.

The participant pathway

One connected plan

The goal is to prevent the familiar gaps that send people back to homelessness, relapse, or incarceration.

1. ConnectBegin planning before release when possible.
2. StabilizeMeet urgent housing, transportation, and basic needs.
3. BuildStart work, training, mentorship, and life-skills development.
4. AdvanceMove toward independence, lasting employment, and healthy community.
Why Broken Growth exists

Built from lived experience

Founder Timothy Oates knows what it means to leave incarceration and face loss, instability, and homelessness. Broken Growth Ministries grew from the conviction that people can feel broken and still grow—and that a second chance needs more than words.

“We want people to leave incarceration with somewhere safe to go, meaningful work to begin, and a community that expects them to succeed.”

Our work is Christian and faith-centered, while practical service, dignity, accountability, and respect remain at the center of every relationship.
